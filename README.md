This paper investigates the development of a Robotic Process Automation (RPA)-based prototype for automated invoice data extraction and its potential implications for financial data analysis. We address the challenge of manual invoice processing by utilizing Microsoft's Power Automate and AI Builder tools. The prototype employs Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to extract relevant financial data from invoices received via email and populate an Excel workbook. We report achieving a 96% accuracy rate in data extraction. This research highlights the potential of AI-driven solutions to enhance the quality and availability of financial datasets, which could significantly impact financial research. We discuss the limitations of the prototype, such as handling foreign currency invoices, and propose avenues for future development, including integration with accounting software via eXtensible Markup Language (XML) conversion.
